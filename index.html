Job Opening: Production Engineer

Derabassi, Punjab Kaptron Plastics & Metals

We are looking for a fresher or experienced CNC professional who can manage production on Turning Centres, VMC, and HMC machines along with quality checks and basic troubleshooting.

Responsibilities

  • Operating and supervising CNC Turning Centres, VMC & HMC.
  • Managing daily production planning and targets.
  • Performing quality checks (Vernier, Micrometer, etc.).
  • Troubleshooting (tool wear, offset correction, alarms).

Requirements

  • ITI / Diploma / B.Tech in Mechanical field.
  • Basic knowledge of CNC programming & offsets.
  • Ability to handle precision measuring instruments.
  • Responsible and disciplined attitude.
